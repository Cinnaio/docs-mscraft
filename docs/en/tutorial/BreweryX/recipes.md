::: tip General Tips
- **Boiling**: Put ingredients into a cauldron with a campfire underneath, fill with water using a bucket, then right-click to add ingredients one by one. Use a clock to check remaining time.
- **Distilling**: Put base wine into a brewing stand, put Glowstone Dust in the fuel slot, and it will automatically distill for the specified number of times.
- **Aging**: Store wine bottles in barrels of the corresponding wood type and wait for the specified years to retrieve.
- **Sealing**: Put wine into the Sealing Table to automatically seal it. After sealing, the wine quality will not change.
- **Wood Type Table**: Oak, Birch, Bamboo, etc. When building barrels, use planks and stairs of the corresponding wood type.
- **Quality & Recovery**: The final quality of the wine affects energy recovery. Strictly following the recipe increases the probability of obtaining high-quality wine, thereby gaining higher recovery bonuses.
:::

For more detailed steps, please refer to the complete [Brewing Guide](brewing-guide.md).
