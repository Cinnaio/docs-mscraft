# Brewer's Guide

Welcome to the tipsy world of **BreweryX**! Here, you will become a true master brewer, personally involved in every drop of the brewing process, from a grain of wheat in the field to the mellow wine aged in barrels. Whether it's refreshing beer, mild Huangjiu (Yellow Wine), or potent Vodka, they are all waiting for you to unlock. Get your cauldron ready, let's get to work!

## Core Concepts

::: info Four Steps of Brewing
Before you start brewing, you need to understand the four core steps:
1.  **Fermenting**: Boil ingredients (such as wheat, berries) in a cauldron to transform them into raw "wort" or "fruit base".
2.  **Distilling**: Purify multiple times through a brewing stand to obtain spirits with higher alcohol content.
3.  **Aging**: Put the wine into a barrel and wait for time to give it color and flavor.
4.  **Sealing**: Put the aged wine into a Sealing Table to lock its quality.
:::

## Preparation: Building Barrels

Barrels are essential tools for aging, and you need to craft them yourself first.

1.  **Collect Materials**: Depending on the wine you want to brew, choose planks of the corresponding wood type (e.g., Oak, Birch, Spruce, etc.) and stairs of the same wood type.
2.  **Build the Barrel**: Place the planks and stairs as shown below:
   
   ![Barrel Construction Diagram](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/21661f34-4a22-4ec5-85eb-6e8974f8ba3a)
   
   * *The yellow/black concrete in the picture is only for length indication, not part of the barrel.*
   * *Large barrels have 27 slots of storage space, small barrels have 9 slots.*

3.  **Activate the Barrel**: After building the main structure of the barrel, place a **Sign** on the barrel and write `barrel` on the first line to successfully create a usable barrel.

## Fermenting Ingredients

### 1. Prepare Ingredients
First, you need to collect the materials required for the recipe. For example, a bottle of basic beer might need:
- 16 Wheat
- A water source (infinite water or a bucket of water)
- A Cauldron
- A heat source (Campfire or Soul Campfire)
- Several Glass Bottles

### 2. Start Boiling
1.  Right-click the cauldron with a **Water Bucket** to fill it.
2.  Place a Campfire under the cauldron.
3.  Hold the ingredients required by the recipe in your hand and right-click to put them into the cauldron one by one.
4.  When **splash particles and bubbles** appear from the cauldron, it means it is fermenting.
5.  Use a **Clock** to right-click the cauldron to check the current boiling time.
6.  After boiling for enough time, hold a **Glass Bottle** and right-click the cauldron to get the base wine.

## Distilling

If you are brewing wine that requires distillation (such as Baijiu, Vodka), please continue with the following steps:

1.  Right-click the **Brewing Stand** to open the interface.
2.  Put the base wine bottle obtained in the previous stage into the bottom slot.
3.  Put **Glowstone Dust** into the ingredient slot (top slot) as filter material (Blaze Powder is not needed).
4.  The distillation process will start automatically and last about 60 seconds.
5.  If the recipe requires multiple distillations, the brewing stand will automatically continue.

::: tip Tip
Insufficient distillation times will result in low alcohol content; too many times may cause the wine to taste bad and quality to drop. Be sure to operate precisely according to the recipe!
:::

## Aging

This step is the key to transforming the wine.

1.  **Confirm Barrel Material**: Ensure the barrel material matches the recipe requirements (e.g., Huangjiu uses Bamboo Planks barrels).
2.  **Store Wine Bottles**: Store the wine in the barrel.
3.  **Wait for Aging**: Aging time is measured in **Minecraft Years** (20 minutes in-game = 1 year). Right-click the barrel to check the status.
4.  **Retrieve Finished Product**: When the best year is reached, right-click the barrel again to retrieve it.

::: warning Note
Insufficient or excessive aging time will affect the final quality. Only the right years can brew "good quality" wine. Be sure to seize the timing to avoid "over-aging".
:::

## Sealing

Take your brewed wine and put it into the Sealing Table, it will automatically be sealed.

![Sealing Table Construction Diagram](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/eb09a713-8f02-44ba-9650-8d1d6cccafbb)

After sealing, the quality of the wine will no longer change, which is very suitable for wine products that need to be transported or sold.

## FAQ

::: details Q: Why is my wine quality always poor?
Check: Is the quantity of ingredients correct? Is the boiling time enough? Is the number of distillations accurate? Is the aging year just right? Every step will affect the final quality.
:::

::: details Q: How long can the wine in the barrel be stored?
Theoretically, it can be stored indefinitely, but after exceeding the best year, the quality will gradually decline. It is recommended to keep track of time and take it out in time.
:::

::: details Q: Do I need to stay online while aging wine?
No, aging is a background process that does not require the player to be online or the chunk where the barrel is located to be loaded.
:::

::: details Q: Can I mass-produce wine?
Of course! You can use multiple cauldrons and multiple barrels to mass-produce at the same time. As long as you have enough ingredients and containers.
:::

::: details Q: How do I check what wines I can brew?
You can find recipes for all public wines in this directory. If you want to know the recipes for some private brews, please consult the recipe holder.
:::
