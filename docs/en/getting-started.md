# Welcome to ClusterGap

Welcome to our world! Here you will experience a unique and vibrant community. Whether you are an architect, explorer, or Redstone engineer, there is a place for you here.

## Player Code of Conduct

To maintain a good gaming environment, please observe the following basic rules:

1. **Friendly Communication**: No insults, harassment, or discrimination against other players.
2. **Fair Play**: Strictly prohibit the use of any form of cheat clients or mods that disrupt game balance.
3. **Respect Labor**: Do not destroy other people's buildings or steal items without permission.
4. **Protect the Environment**: Do not engage in meaningless terrain destruction or high-frequency Redstone lag machines.

*For more detailed player rules, please refer to [Server Rules](server-faq.md).*
